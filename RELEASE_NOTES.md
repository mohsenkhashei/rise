# Rise v1.0.0 Release Notes

Welcome to the initial release of **Rise**, a clean, minimalist, and elegant new tab replacement browser extension designed to bring focus and inspiration to your day. 

## What's Included

*   **Fliqlo-Style Flip Clock**: A beautiful, premium retro-style flipping clock that keeps you on track.
*   **Daily Rotating Wallpapers**: Stunning, curated high-resolution backgrounds fetched dynamically from the public `rise-data` repository.
*   **Daily Quotes**: Hand-picked, inspiring quotes updated daily to start your session with the right mindset.
*   **Minimalist Settings Panel**: A clean overlay that lets you customize your dashboard. Toggle the visibility of the clock, wallpapers, and quotes to suit your preferences.
*   **Privacy by Design**: Rise collects no telemetry, tracking, or personal data. Your preferences and daily choices are cached entirely in local browser storage.

## How to Install

### 🌐 Chrome / Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi)
1. Visit the [Chrome Web Store Listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/placeholder-id-here) (coming soon).
2. Click **Add to Chrome**.

### 🦊 Firefox
1. Visit the [Firefox Add-ons Listing](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/placeholder-name-here) (coming soon).
2. Click **Add to Firefox**.

### 🛠️ Manual Installation (Developer Mode)
If you wish to install the extension manually:
1. Download the release zip from this page.
2. Unzip the file on your computer.
3. Open your browser's extensions page (`chrome://extensions` or `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`).
4. Enable **Developer Mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked** (Chrome) or **Load Temporary Add-on** (Firefox) and select the unzipped directory.
